🏷️ Google Tag (Demo)

This is how a Google Ads conversion tracking tag looks. Copy & paste in your website's <head> section:

<!-- Google tag (gtag.js) -->
<script async src="https://www.googletagmanager.com/gtag/js?id=AW-DEMO123"></script>
<script>
  window.dataLayer = window.dataLayer || [];
  function gtag(){dataLayer.push(arguments);}
  gtag('js', new Date());
  gtag('config', 'AW-DEMO123');
</script>

<!-- Event snippet for Purchase -->
<script>
  gtag('event', 'conversion', {
    'send_to': 'AW-DEMO123/abcDEF',
    'value': 1499.0,
    'currency': 'INR'
  });
</script>
